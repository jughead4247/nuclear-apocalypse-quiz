Could You Survive a Nuclear Apocalypse?

A nuclear apocalypse isn't simply about surviving the initial blast. Shelter, fallout, water, food, information, cooperation and decision-making could determine whether you survive for days, months or years.

In this 50-decision survival test, you'll face difficult situations involving nuclear fallout, shelter, supplies, other survivors and the choices you would have to make when resources become scarce.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Nuclear Apocalypse Survival Quiz work?

You'll face 50 difficult decisions covering shelter, fallout, water, food, supplies, survivors, cooperation and morality. Your choices determine your final fictional survival score.

What is the maximum score?

Your final survival score is displayed as a percentage, with 100% representing the highest possible survival score.

What does my survival score mean?

Your score represents how well your decisions performed within the fictional nuclear apocalypse scenario presented by the quiz.

What is the morality score?

Some decisions also evaluate the moral choices you make during the scenario. Your morality score reflects those choices within the fictional situation.

Is this a real nuclear survival test?

No. This quiz is fictional entertainment and is not a substitute for official emergency guidance or professional disaster preparedness advice.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after completing the quiz to replay the scenario and see how different choices affect your result.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the Challenge Someone button to share your result and compare your survival score.
