About the Nuclear Apocalypse Survival Quiz

Could you survive a nuclear apocalypse? This 50-decision survival quiz puts you in a series of difficult situations following a nuclear catastrophe and tests how effectively you respond.

Your decisions cover critical survival situations involving shelter, fallout, radiation, water, food, medical supplies, information, other survivors, cooperation and difficult moral choices.

Each decision affects your overall survival score. Some decisions also affect your morality score, showing how your survival choices compare with your willingness to help or sacrifice for others.

This quiz is created for entertainment and educational purposes. It is not a substitute for official emergency guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Nuclear Apocalypse Survival Quiz work?

You will face 50 survival decisions based on situations that could arise after a nuclear catastrophe. Choose the option you believe gives you the best chance of surviving. Your choices are then used to calculate your survival and morality scores.

How many decisions are in the quiz?

The quiz contains 50 decisions. Each situation presents four possible choices, and you must select one before moving to the next decision.

What does the survival score mean?

Your survival score represents how effectively your decisions address the challenges presented throughout the scenario. A higher score indicates stronger overall survival decision-making.

What is the morality score?

The morality score reflects the moral dimension of your decisions. Some choices prioritize your own survival, while others involve helping, protecting or sacrificing for other people.

What does the estimated survival time mean?

Your estimated survival is the result assigned by the quiz based on your overall performance. It is part of the fictional survival scenario and should not be interpreted as a scientific prediction of how long someone would actually survive after a nuclear disaster.

What topics does the quiz cover?

The decisions cover subjects such as nuclear fallout, shelter, radiation exposure, water, food, supplies, medical needs, communication, information, group survival, resource management and encounters with other survivors.

Are the situations based on real nuclear disasters?

The quiz uses realistic survival concepts as inspiration, but the individual situations and decisions are presented as a fictional apocalypse scenario. The quiz is designed to test decision-making and provide an entertaining survival challenge rather than predict real-world events.

Can I take the quiz again?

Yes. Select TRY AGAIN after receiving your result to restart the quiz and see whether you can achieve a higher survival score or make different decisions.

Can I challenge my friends?

Yes. After completing the quiz, use the CHALLENGE SOMEONE button to share your result and challenge your friends to see how they would survive the same nuclear apocalypse scenario.
